What Standard Grow does

Standard Grow, by Standard Trade, is a number you text a reel to. It sends back ads and a budget for your business, runs them once you approve, and reports what they brought in. Part of the service publishes approved content to TikTok and Instagram accounts you connect. If you connect Gmail, it can also send outreach emails you approve, from your own address.

This policy explains what Standard Grow collects and how it is used. Standard Grow is operated by Standard Trade.

What we collect

  • What you text us. The reels, links and business details you send us over iMessage or WhatsApp, and the number or account you send them from.
  • Your email. If you leave it on this site instead of texting us.
  • Account connection data. When you connect a TikTok or Instagram account, we receive an access token and basic profile information (such as your account ID and username) from that platform.
  • Content you schedule. The images, captions and posting times you set up.
  • Publishing records. When each post was sent and the status the platform returned.
  • Gmail connection (optional). If you connect Gmail, Google gives us your email address and a token that lets us send email as you. We ask only for send access (the gmail.send permission). We cannot read, search or delete your mail.
  • Emails you approve. Each email Standard Grow drafts for you: who it is to, the subject, the text, and whether it was sent.

How we use it

We use what you text us only to prepare your experiments. Reel files you send us are deleted after 1 day.

Account connection data, scheduled content and publishing records are used only to publish your content to the accounts you connected, at the times you chose, and to show you what was posted. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the platform you are posting to.

Google user data

We use the Gmail connection only to send the emails you approve in your chat with Standard Grow. Each email is shown to you in full before it goes out, and nothing is sent without your yes. We keep a record of each email so the same person is never emailed twice.

Standard Grow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to train AI models, and we do not share it with anyone except as needed to send your emails or as the law requires. No person at Standard Trade reads it, except with your permission, for security, or as the law requires.

Site analytics

This site uses PostHog to count page views and sign-ups for each version of the page, so we can see which works best. Your email is not sent to PostHog.

Storage and security

Tokens and content are stored on the system that runs Standard Grow, with access limited to the operator. Images are served only so the platform can fetch them for your post.

Retention and deletion

Reel files you send us are deleted after 1 day. Disconnecting an account deletes its token. You can disconnect Gmail at any time from your Google account permissions, or by asking us; we then delete the Gmail token. You can ask us to delete all of your data at any time by emailing info@standardtrade.ai. You can also revoke access from TikTok's, Instagram's or Google's own settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
