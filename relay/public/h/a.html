Questions

What is Standard Grow?

Standard Grow, by Standard Trade, is a number you text about your business. Every day it brings you one thing that moves the business forward, already made, so you just say yes.

What happens when I text it?

Send your website or Instagram. Grow reads it the way a buyer would, tells you one thing your posts leave out, and asks you one question before it makes anything.

What does it make?

Posts and stories from your own photos, and emails to people who could buy from you. Nothing goes out until you say yes.

Can I send it a reel or post I liked?

Yes. Text the link and Grow makes your version of it for your business.

Do I need an app or a marketing team?

No. It runs from your phone over text. You decide what goes out.

What does it cost?

Your first campaign is free.

Who is it for?

Owners of small businesses: online stores, local shops, apps and creators selling a product.
