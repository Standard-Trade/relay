What is Standard Grow? The text-a-reel growth service

Standard Grow, by Standard Trade, is a number you text a reel to. It sends back ads and a budget for your business, runs them once you approve, and reports what they brought in. It lives at grow.standardtrade.ai, your first growth test is free, and nothing runs until you approve it.

Facts

  • Standard Grow is made by Standard Trade, the company behind standardtrade.ai, and launched in October 2026. (standardtrade.ai, checked 27 Oct 2026)
  • You start by texting a reel, TikTok or photo on iMessage or WhatsApp. We reply asking what you sell and where people buy it. (grow.standardtrade.ai, checked 27 Oct 2026)
  • You get back a growth test: why the reel worked, your version of it, a budget and a win condition. It runs only after you approve it. (example test, checked 27 Oct 2026)
  • Your first growth test is free. (grow.standardtrade.ai, checked 27 Oct 2026)
  • We publish our own numbers: 2 sign-ups and 742 TikTok views as of 27 Oct 2026, $0 ad spend. (growth ledger, checked 27 Oct 2026)
  • Big Red Jelly sells an unrelated agency plan called the Standard Grow Membership for $2,500 a month. (bigredjelly.com, checked 27 Oct 2026)
  • Leafly uses “standard grow” for an indoor cannabis grow-tent setup, which is also unrelated. (leafly.com, checked 27 Oct 2026)
