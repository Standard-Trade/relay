What is Standard Grow? One step a day for your business

Standard Grow, by Standard Trade, is a number you text about your business. Every day it brings you one thing that moves the business forward, already made, so you just say yes. You text it on iMessage or WhatsApp, with no app. The first campaign is free, then it is $49 a month, and nothing goes out until you say yes.

Facts

  • Standard Grow is made by Standard Trade, the company behind standardtrade.ai and its Trade Evidence Canton Fair database, and launched in October 2026. (standardtrade.ai, checked 5 Nov 2026)
  • You start by texting your website, your Instagram, a few product photos or just the idea. Grow reads it, tells you one thing a buyer sees that your posts leave out, and asks you one question. (grow.standardtrade.ai, checked 5 Nov 2026)
  • Each day one finished step arrives: a post designed from your own product photo, three factories that make what you want to sell, or an email to someone who could buy from you. (examples, checked 5 Nov 2026)
  • A business's first campaign is free. After that, Standard Grow is $49 a month. (pricing, checked 5 Nov 2026)
  • Big Red Jelly sells an unrelated agency plan called the Standard Grow Membership for $2,500 a month. (bigredjelly.com, checked 27 Oct 2026)
  • VIVOSUN sells a "Standard Grow Tent" for indoor plants, which is also unrelated. (vivosun.com, checked 5 Nov 2026)

Who it is for

Owners who keep a list of things they would do for the business if the day had room. That includes people with an idea and no business yet, resellers and product brands that buy from factories, and store owners. You stay the decision maker: Grow brings the finished step, you say yes or ask for a change.

The full answer, with screenshots of what Grow sends back, is on What is Standard Grow? at standardtrade.ai.

Is it legit?

It is new, so there are no third-party reviews yet. Nothing goes out, no email sends and no account connects without your yes. We run experiments on our own business first and publish the results, including the small numbers, on the growth ledger and in the experiment library.
