Standard Grow

Slideshow posts for your own accounts, on schedule.

Standard Grow is a publishing tool. You write or approve photo slideshows, connect the TikTok and Instagram accounts you own, and Standard Grow posts each slideshow at the time you set.

How it works

  1. Connect each of your accounts by signing in with TikTok or Instagram.
  2. Set each account's posting times and a daily limit.
  3. Standard Grow sends each slideshow to TikTok or Instagram at its time. On TikTok, drafts go to your inbox so you can add a sound and publish.

Every account starts slowly and posting stops automatically if a platform reports a problem.
